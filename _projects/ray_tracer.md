For my undergraduate class, CS 488, introduction to graphics, we had a big final project where we were able to choose to build something graphics related. I decided to opt into a ray tracer, where I made a Japanese temple inspired by 金閣寺 (Kinkaku-ji), meaning golden temple found in Kyoto. This included many different features including reflections, normal mapping, constructive solid geometry, L-system trees, etc.
